# Contact Us

We'd love to hear from you. Whether it's a question about a review, a product suggestion, a correction, or a business inquiry, here is how to reach us:

## Product review requests / samples

If you are a manufacturer or PR agency and would like us to consider a product for review, please email us with:

- Product name and key specs
- Suggested selling price and launch date
- Whether a review unit or sample is available
- Any deadlines

> Note: receiving a free sample never guarantees a positive review. We disclose all sponsored or sample-based content clearly, in line with our [editorial policy](/about/).
