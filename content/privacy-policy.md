# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **TBblog** ("we", "us", "our") — operating at `https://blog.toolsboot.com` — collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Information you provide voluntarily

- **Contact form / email**: if you contact us, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to reply to you.
- We do **not** require registration and we do **not** collect account data.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we may automatically collect technical information such as:

- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Device type
- Pages visited, referring URL and time of visit
- IP address (in anonymized or aggregated form, where possible)

This data is used for analytics, security and to improve our content.

## 2. Cookies

Our site uses cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

- **Essential cookies**: required for basic site functionality.
- **Analytics**: we currently do not use third-party analytics services that set cookies on your device. If we enable analytics in the future, we will update this policy to name the provider.
- **Advertising / affiliate cookies**: used by third parties (see below) to track referrals and purchases.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features.

## 3. Third-party services

### 3.1 Amazon Associates (affiliate program)

TBblog participates in the **Amazon Services LLC Associates Program**, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

When you click an Amazon affiliate link on our site, Amazon may place cookies on your browser to track your visit and any purchases. This allows us to earn a commission at **no extra cost to you**. Amazon's data practices are governed by [Amazon's own Privacy Notice](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=468496).

### 3.2 Hosting & CDN

Our site is hosted on **Cloudflare Pages**. Cloudflare processes data (including IP addresses) to deliver the site securely and may set security-related cookies. See [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details.

### 3.3 Analytics

If we enable analytics, we will use privacy-friendly tools and update this section accordingly.

## 4. How we use your information

We use collected information to:

- Operate and maintain the website
- Improve content quality and user experience
- Understand affiliate performance (aggregated)
- Prevent abuse and ensure security
- Respond to your inquiries

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 5. Data retention

We retain personal data (e.g. email correspondence) only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry or as required by law. Automated logs are kept for security purposes and deleted periodically.

## 6. Children's privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
